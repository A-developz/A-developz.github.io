Mac n Cheese Recipe

Meaning To Our Family

This is a comfort food that we enjoy every single holiday season. It is the ultimate side dish for dinners, but it also packs perfectly for weekday school lunches. My brother commented on this recipe as, It's the creamiest mac n cheese ever, and it disappears within five minutes of hitting the table.

Ingredients

Ingredients for 4 to 6 people. Prep time is 20 to 30 minutes.

macaroni pasta 400g
sharp cheddar cheese 300g
butter 4 tablespoons
all-purpose flour 4 tablespoons
whole milk 500ml
heavy cream 120ml
salt 1 teaspoon
black pepper 1/2 teaspoon
mustard powder 1/2 teaspoon

Instructions

  1. Boil the macaroni pasta in salted water until al dente, then drain and set aside.
  2. Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat, whisk in the flour, and cook for 1 minute to form a roux.
  3. Gradually pour in the milk and heavy cream, whisking constantly until the mixture thickens into a smooth sauce.
  4. Turn off the heat, stir in the cheese and spices until completely melted, then mix in the pasta and serve!

Tips and Possible Substitutions

  • The sharp cheddar cheese could be substituted by Gruyere or Gouda.
  • More cheese on top for baking, the better.
  • The pasta would be best if it was freshly boiled.
