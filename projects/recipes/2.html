Biscoff Cheesecake Recipe

Origins of this recipe

I am going to be completely honest with you, there is no exciting origin or history behind this recipe. It was just conjured but by a hungry and sleep deprived version of me when I fast in ramadan.

Ingredients

Ingredients for 6 to 8 people. Prep time is 30 minutes (plus chilling time).

Lotus Biscoff cookies (crushed) 250g
unsalted butter (melted) 80g
cream cheese (softened) 600g
powdered sugar 100g
Lotus Biscoff spread 200g
heavy cream 200ml

Instructions

  1. Make the crust: Mix the crushed Lotus Biscoff cookies with the melted unsalted butter. Press the mixture firmly into the bottom of a lined springform pan. Chill in the fridge for 10 minutes.
  2. Prepare the filling: Beat the softened cream cheese and powdered sugar together in a large bowl until smooth and creamy.
  3. Add the Biscoff spread: Gently fold or swirl in the Lotus Biscoff spread until well combined with the cream cheese mixture.
  4. Assemble: Spread the cheesecake mixture evenly over the chilled cookie crust.
  5. Chill and serve: Refrigerate for at least 4 hours (or overnight) until firm before slicing and serving.
